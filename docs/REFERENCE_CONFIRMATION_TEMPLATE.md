# Manager confirmation template

The candidate may prepare a factual draft, but the manager should correct it, remove anything they cannot verify, confirm what may be disclosed, and sign only the final approved wording.

Do not list SQL Server, Terraform, Azure or Databricks as professional technologies unless the manager directly observed their use in the role.

## Suggested fact sheet for manager review

- Employee: Oksana Kolisnyk
- Role: Business Data Analyst
- Employment period: to be confirmed by HR/manager
- Domain: media/content performance analytics and reporting
- Responsibilities to confirm:
  - data preparation and validation;
  - recurring performance reporting and dashboard/workbook delivery;
  - reporting automation using approved Python, SQL and spreadsheet workflows;
  - KPI logic for views, revenue and audience retention;
  - metadata registry/mapping and reporting-scope controls;
  - collaboration with business/report consumers.
- Technologies to confirm only if accurate: Python, SQL, Power BI, Looker Studio, Excel, Google Sheets, platform analytics APIs.
- Confidentiality: no customer data, source code, credentials, internal URLs or exact confidential values are disclosed.

## Approval process

1. Send the manager the fact sheet, not a pre-signed claim.
2. Ask them to edit the technology and responsibility lists.
3. Confirm whether HR/legal approval is required.
4. Agree whether their contact details may be shared privately with recruiters.
5. Keep the signed letter private unless the manager explicitly approves publication.
