# Evidence standard

## Evidence labels

- **Professional:** performed in employment or contracted work; disclosure may be limited.
- **Academy case:** completed selection/education task with shareable deliverables.
- **Independent lab:** self-directed project using synthetic/public data.
- **Learning:** exercises, notes or interview drills; not delivery experience.
- **Planned:** not yet evidence and not listed as a current skill.

## Minimum case structure

Every featured case should state:

1. business problem;
2. personal contribution;
3. technology actually used;
4. quality or validation approach;
5. outcome without invented metrics;
6. evidence link;
7. confidentiality and verification boundary.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not convert a lab into a client project through wording.
- Do not list a platform because it appears in a job ad.
- Do not publish internal data, code or screenshots under NDA.
- Do not invent scale, savings, latency or business impact.
- Do not call a workflow green before it completes successfully.
