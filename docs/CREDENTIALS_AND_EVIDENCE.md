## 📜 Courses and certifications

No course completion or certification is listed here until it can be named accurately with its provider, completion date and a shareable credential link or a privately held certificate. This avoids turning planned study into a claimed qualification.

When a credential is ready, use this format:

| Credential | Provider | Completed | Verification | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example only — do not treat as completed | Provider | YYYY-MM | Public URL or “private certificate available on request” | One sentence |

## ✅ Label standard

- **Professional context** — performed in a real work setting; public description is anonymized.
- **Independent portfolio project** — original, inspectable code with synthetic/public data; never presented as a client engagement.
- **Academy case** — completed educational or selection case with shareable deliverables.
- **Learning artifact** — practice, coursework, drill or exploration; not used as delivery evidence.
- **Verified credential** — a completed course/certification with a provider, date and a verification route.
