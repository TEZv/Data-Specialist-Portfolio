# Case 2 — TaskFlow Product Turnaround

**Classification:** Product Analytics / Business Analytics

**Context:** Genesis Academy Strategy & Operations School 2.0 case task

**Evidence:** public calculation workbook, presentation, sources and speaking guide

## Problem

A subscription productivity product showed a broad decline across downloads, MAU, paying users, retention and MRR. The task was to distinguish market pressure from product problems and recommend a defensible 12-month direction.

## Analytical work

- Diagnosed the decline using product and business metrics.
- Compared acquisition, activation, retention, churn and monetization signals.
- Reviewed competitor positioning and feature gaps.
- Assessed strategic alternatives and risks.
- Built a KPI-based turnaround roadmap.
- Connected recommendations to D30/D90 retention, paid churn, organic share and MRR recovery.
